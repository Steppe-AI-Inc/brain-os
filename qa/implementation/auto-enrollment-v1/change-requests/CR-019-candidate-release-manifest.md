**CR-019 — at candidate time, before any certifying receipt exists, which artifact is "the candidate's release manifest" whose
per-channel digest AC-5(h) and VERIFICATION_SPEC §3.8 compare the verifier's reproduced digest with? (AC-5(h); §3.8; WO-6
"Manifest"; WO-10 release-provenance inputs)**

- **Filed:** 2026-09-29 by the IMPLEMENTER capability, on `factory/auto-enrollment-v1-implementation` (release-verification area,
  L7-08).
- **Built on:** Director r3 `c7a845b61a3b0b419e8c9dfeff397547fdc75b03`.
- **Kind:** a clarification of how a Director criterion is read. No product behaviour depends on it, and no Director text is edited.
- **Until the Director decides, the candidate keeps the r3 behaviour** and relies on no answer: the candidate report tabulates the

## Requirement (the r3 texts)

- AC-5(h): "Each channel's reproduced digest equals that channel's digest in the candidate's release manifest."
- VERIFICATION_SPEC §3.8: "Each channel's digest must equal that channel's digest in the candidate's release manifest."
- WO-6, Manifest: "Source SHA / version, artifact digest, signing key id, signature, and the hash of the Director receipt that
  certified the candidate."
- VERIFICATION_SPEC §2 lists the contents of the candidate notice; a release manifest is not among them.

## What the candidate does

- `scripts/factory-runner/enrolled/release.mjs` defines the WO-6 manifest: `{ v: 1, channel, version, source_sha, digest, key_id,
  receipt_sha256, signature }`, with the signature over the other fields. `receipt_sha256` is the hash of the certifying receipt,
  so a WO-6 manifest of a candidate cannot exist before that candidate is certified; a manifest with the key id, signature and
  receipt hash left empty is refused by every runtime (malformed / unsigned).
- A manifest, or any statement of the digest, cannot be inside the frozen SHA's own tree: build-sea compiles the source commit and
  its commit time into the exe (`__BUILD_INFO__`), so a commit cannot hold the digest of an exe built from itself.
- `CANDIDATE_REPORT.md` ("Release provenance inputs", generated by `qa/implementation/auto-enrollment-v1/tools/provenance.mjs`)
  tabulates per channel: runtime version, source commit, dirty, built_at, the digest (PE Authenticode image hash), the unsigned file
  sha256, the trust mode, every trust entry as (key id, sha256 of the public key), and the default endpoint.
- New in this area: `provenance.mjs --emit <dir>` writes `<channel>.build-declaration.json` for both channels from a clean build of
  HEAD (it refuses a dirty build, a build of another commit, or an exe whose image hash is not its build-info digest), and
  `provenance.mjs --check <dir>` rebuilds both channels from the checked-out commit and compares every declared field (MATCH / exit 0,
  DIFFERENT / exit 1). Each declaration states that it is not a WO-6 release manifest. The declarations are emitted from a clean
  checkout of the frozen SHA and committed in the candidate-notice commit (a child of the frozen SHA that changes no build input),
  under `qa/implementation/auto-enrollment-v1/evidence/<frozen sha>/build-declarations/`; `final_pass.mjs --declarations <dir>` runs
  the check.
- Clarified after the verifier review, before this branch was published (the wording is in `provenance.mjs` as committed in
  `0822f27`): each declaration calls itself the implementer's WO-10 release-provenance
  evidence, information only, not presented as the candidate's release manifest of AC-5(h) / §3.8 unless the Director so decides;
  the notice presents them that way (VERIFICATION_SPEC §2 fixes the notice contents). `--check` compares the declared fields;
  `build_info_sha256` is informational and not compared (it hashes the signing fields too, and a `--check` rebuild is never signed).
  Because the notice commit is a child of the declared commit, `provenance.mjs --check` and `final_pass.mjs --declarations` run in a
  clean checkout of the frozen SHA with the declarations copied to a directory outside the tree; both refuse (exit 2) a declaration
  of another commit, `final_pass.mjs` before any step.

## Why a decision is needed

Which artifact counts as "the candidate's release manifest" for AC-5(h) and §3.8 is a reading of a Director criterion. The
implementer may propose a reading but not adopt one (CLAUDE.md §8). The r3 texts can be read at least four ways (below), and they
lead to different notice contents and a different comparison for the verifier.

## Alternatives (for the Director)

1. **(Proposed)** The per-channel build declarations committed in the candidate-notice commit, cited in the notice by path and
   sha256, are "the candidate's release manifest" for the purpose of AC-5(h) / §3.8 at candidate stage; the WO-6 manifest (with key
   id, signature and receipt hash) exists only from certification on.
2. The candidate report's provenance table alone.
3. A WO-6-shaped manifest per channel, with key id, signature and receipt hash left empty until certification, committed in the
   notice commit (never installable: every runtime refuses it).
4. AC-5(h) applies at certification only: the verifier records its reproduced digests at candidate stage, and the comparison is made
   against the WO-6 manifest the founder signs after the receipt.

## Impact

- Product code: none under any alternative.
- The candidate-notice procedure: under (1) the notice commit carries the declarations and cites them; under (3) it carries the
  empty-signature manifests instead; under (2) and (4) nothing is added.
- Meanwhile the candidate provides (1)'s declarations and (2)'s table. It relies on no answer.
