- **Binding**, revision 1, issued by the DIRECTOR.
- Contract: §3 P-1 / P-8 / P-10, §11; `VERIFICATION_SPEC.md` §3.
- Founder text: II.7, II.9, I A.3 §3, I A.4 §12.
- Executed by: the IMPLEMENTER.
- Certified by: a distinct authorized verifier.
- Scope changes: only a Director revision changes this WO.

## What must be true
- **Factory V1 accounting is cumulative. This is a BINDING ACCEPTANCE REQUIREMENT, not a founder gate** (II.9).
  - The V1 record is **acceptance state, recorded by the Director** (AC-13, which is Director-owned and not a WO row).
  - The candidate supplies only evidence inputs: its release provenance, and the exact release SHA its AC-1..AC-4 evidence will carry.
  - It never re-runs, recreates or rewrites the `69df2f52` baseline evidence (the manifest), and never decides which V1 gates exist
    (G-1..G-6, contract P-8).
- **Certified reference suites.** They are green on the candidate. The list is in `VERIFICATION_SPEC.md` §3.7. A successor counts only
  under a Director-ratified change request, with a row-by-row mapping.
- **No founder poke** (P-10). The certified `founder_poke_not_required.mjs`, or a ratified successor, passes.
- **Baseline intact.**
  - `factory/computer-agnostic-control-plane` = `69df2f52`.
  - The manifest's evidence fields are byte-identical after any migration.
- **Isolation (S-15).** Candidate tests never touch:
  - the live legacy checkout `C:\Users\DELL\dev\brain-os-factory-cp` (the feature branch is never checked out there);
  - the live task;
  - `~/.brain-factory/runner.env`;
  - the live plane;
  - production;
  - `master`;
  - the acceptance machine.
- **No production actions and no secrets.**
  - Nothing is performed; every founder action is prepared.
  - The secret scan is clean.
- **QA authority is enforceable.**
  - The implementer never writes receipts or acceptance states, never seeds or relaxes a policy beyond the Director rows, and never
    self-closes.
  - Unratified change requests are not implemented against.
- **Authorship provenance** (`VERIFICATION_SPEC.md` §1, §3.1).
  - The implementer generates an SSH commit-signing key on the implementing machine and publishes its public key in a change request.
    The Director records it in the ledger as `implementer_signing_key`.
  - Every commit of every candidate is signed with that key. The private key never leaves the implementing machine.
  - The candidate's canonical-document copies are byte-identical to the designated Director commit's.
- **Provenance.**
  - `CAPABILITY_IMPACT_REGISTRY.yaml` gains the Factory entries.
  - The candidate report lists every table, function, route, secret name and environment variable.
  - The candidate names the Director commit it was built against.
  - It stops at DEV-VERIFIED → exact candidate SHA frozen → branch `factory/auto-enrollment-v1-implementation` pushed → READY FOR
    INDEPENDENT QA. It never self-certifies.
